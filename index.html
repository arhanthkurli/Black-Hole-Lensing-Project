How it works

Light is traced backward from each pixel to the sky. Rays captured by the black hole form the shadow. Rays that loop around it can bring the same region of sky into view more than once.

Sky octants

Each color marks one of eight regions of the sky. The camera sits 20 M from the black hole and points toward it.
